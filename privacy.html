In brief: The Extension processes financial and document information locally in your browser to find original PDFs and create ZIP or CSV exports you request. 2. Information handled and why

After you give affirmative consent in the Extension and use it on a supported Scalable Capital page, it reads information needed to provide its PDF-download and export features. Information is obtained from the Scalable Capital page and responses, documents you select, settings you choose, and support messages you send us. Depending on your account and choices, it can include:

Information categoryExamples and purpose
Financial and account informationTransaction records, dates, transaction types, security or document names, account-related identifiers and document availability. Used to search, filter, display and match documents for exports you request.
Document informationDocument names, metadata, document URLs and selected original PDF files. Used to locate, verify and include documents in a ZIP archive.

Support informationIf you email Prompt Monkey, your email address, message, and attachments. Used to respond and handle the support request.

The Extension does not ask for or store your Scalable Capital password and does not request Chrome’s cookie permission or read cookie values through that permission. Requests to Scalable Capital use your existing signed-in browser session. Information is not used for advertising, profiling, sale, or unrelated purposes. The Extension does not make decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

3. Where information goes and who receives it

  • Your browser: The Extension processes account and document information locally. It does not send transaction lists, PDFs, ZIP archives, or CSV exports to Prompt Monkey. It has no developer-operated account-data server and no analytics or advertising integration.
  • Scalable Capital: Your browser sends requests to Scalable Capital using your existing signed-in session to retrieve the records and documents you request. Scalable Capital receives those requests and processes them under its own terms and privacy notices.
  • Document hosts: A PDF URL supplied by Scalable Capital may point to a host on another domain. Your browser requests the selected PDF from that host. The host may receive the requested URL, your IP address, ordinary connection information, and any referrer information your browser sends. For external document hosts, the Extension omits Scalable Capital credentials and cookies from the PDF request. The host’s own practices govern its processing.
  • Support email: If you contact Prompt Monkey, email providers may process the message and delivery information needed to send, receive, and retain it.

Prompt Monkey does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, targeted advertising, or profiling. The Extension does not transfer account records to Prompt Monkey. Chrome and your device may process information as part of their own browser, operating-system, backup, or security functions; those activities are governed by their providers’ terms and settings.

4. Storage, retention, and deletion

  • Temporary Extension data: Transaction and document information, result lists, verification/status data, duplicate-download history, and temporary request templates are held in Chrome’s storage.session. Chrome keeps this storage in memory while the Extension is loaded. It is cleared when Chrome restarts or the Extension is disabled, reloaded, or updated. Request templates are separated by Scalable Capital tab and removed when that tab closes. This data is not stored in Chrome Sync.
  • Local settings: Chrome’s local Extension storage holds language and filter preferences and the minimum consent or withdrawal status needed to operate the consent gate. It remains until you clear the Extension’s data or remove the Extension. It is not transaction history and is not stored in Chrome Sync.
  • Consent withdrawal: The Extension removes the consent grant version and timestamp, retains a withdrawal marker so it remains blocked until you consent again, and clears temporary session account data.
  • Files you download: ZIP and CSV exports remain in the location managed by Chrome until you delete them. The Extension does not remotely access or automatically delete downloaded files.
  • Email support: Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer and follow up on your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

You can clear local Extension data through Chrome or remove the Extension. After withdrawal, reload open Scalable Capital tabs to end the Extension’s page integration.

5. Security

The Extension uses secure web connections for requests to Scalable Capital. It limits saved request headers to a fixed allowlist and does not retain authentication headers or cookies in request templates. Chrome’s storage.session is held in memory while the Extension is loaded and is not persisted to disk by that API. Information being processed during an active browser session may still be accessible to software or people who can access your device or browser profile.

The Extension does not add encryption or password protection to ZIP or CSV files. Those exports may contain sensitive financial information in readable form. Store them only on a device and in a location you control, use device-level protections, and delete copies you no longer need. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Consent withdrawal

You can withdraw consent at any time from the Extension’s privacy panel by selecting “Withdraw consent.” The Extension immediately blocks further use, attempts to stop its page integration, cancels an in-progress ZIP download where possible, and clears temporary account and session data. It removes the consent grant record while retaining the withdrawal marker and language and filter settings locally. To use the Extension again, you must provide consent again. Withdrawal does not affect processing completed before withdrawal, and files already downloaded remain on your device for you to delete.
